**BEFORE BUYING — PLEASE READ**

- **Requires a compatible rootless jailbreak.** Intended for iOS 16–26; compatibility has not been verified on every device or version.
- **Charging pause and charger bypass are not guaranteed.** Both tests currently fail on our iPhone XR with iOS 18.4. These features depend on your battery driver; do not buy solely for charge limiting or bypass on that setup.
- **Separate rootless and RootHide builds.** Install the version matching your jailbreak. The RootHide build is not yet device-tested.
- Available readings and system actions vary by device and iOS version. Battery health and time remaining are system readings or estimates.

# MyLittleEnergy — Free

**Your battery. Your control.**

Give your battery a dashboard of its own. Track what matters, spot changes over time and make your battery display yours — all in one app.

- **Live insights:** charge level, temperature, power, health, cycle count and charger details.
- **Useful history:** explore 24-hour, 7-day, 30-day and 90-day views, review charging sessions and export CSV.
- **Your look:** status-bar readings, battery icon colors, a movable live HUD, charging animations and Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets.
- **Charging tools:** configurable limits, temperature protection, full-charge schedules and a cancellable “Charge to 100% Once” exception, where driver support allows.
- **Alerts and automation:** battery and temperature alerts with sounds or speech, rules for supported system actions and Shortcuts integration.

English and German • Accent colors • °C / °F. The optional Control Center toggle requires CCSupport.

**Free. No account, activation or DRM.** Battery history stays on your device.
